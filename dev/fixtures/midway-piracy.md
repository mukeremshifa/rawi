# Lecture 12: Piracy and maritime law

Fictional lecture notes for a midway upload. They bring subjects the earlier materials never name.

## The rise of the buccaneers

**Buccaneers** were raiders who hunted cattle on Hispaniola before turning to plunder at sea in the 1630s. Their crews elected captains and divided prizes by written articles, which fixed each share before a voyage began.

## Letters of marque

A **letter of marque** was a licence from a government that allowed a private ship to seize enemy vessels in wartime. A privateer holding one was treated as a combatant, while a pirate without one could be hanged. The distinction often depended on which court heard the case.

## The suppression campaign

After 1717 the crown offered a pardon to pirates who surrendered within a year. Naval patrols followed, and by 1726 the fictional Admiralty court at Port Calloway had tried more than 400 men. Historians disagree on whether the pardon or the patrols did more to end the golden age of piracy.
