# Week 3 notes: Functionalism

Fictional lecture notes for a midway upload to the course outlined from its syllabus.

## Functional roles

**Functionalism** is the view that a mental state is defined by what it does: its causes, its effects on other mental states and its effects on behaviour. Pain, on this view, is whatever state is caused by damage and causes avoidance.

## Multiple realisability

If a mental state is a functional role, the same state could be realised by different physical systems. A human brain, an octopus nervous system and, in principle, a machine could all be in pain. This argument was aimed at the identity theory, which tied each mental state to one kind of brain state.

## A side note on embodied cognition

**Embodied cognition** holds that thinking depends on the body and its surroundings, not on the brain alone. It is not on the syllabus, but it is the strongest recent challenge to the functionalist picture.
